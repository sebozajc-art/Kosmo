Izjava o zasebnosti – Kosmo

Velja od 2. novembra 2026
SlovenščinaEnglish

Kosmo je aplikacija za mizarske delavnice: skeniraš etiketo kosa in v 3D modelu vidiš, kje stoji. Ta izjava pojasnjuje, katere podatke aplikacija obdeluje in zakaj.

Kateri podatki se shranijo

  • Račun: e-naslov in geslo (geslo je shranjeno šifrirano), po želji tvoje ime, ki ga vidijo sodelavci v isti delavnici.
  • Delavnica: ime delavnice in seznam članov.
  • Projekti: datoteke, ki jih uvoziš (npr. iz 3D Corpusa ali Mega Tischlerja), s kosi, merami, materiali in okovjem.
  • Delo v delavnici: kateri kosi so poskenirani, kdo in kdaj; kosi za ponoven razrez z opombo in fotografijo, ki jo dodaš sam; opravila z rokom.
  • Obvestila: če jih vklopiš, se shrani tehnični naslov tvoje naprave za pošiljanje obvestil.

Kamera

Kamera se uporablja samo za branje črtnih in QR kod na etiketah. Slika se obdela na napravi in se ne shrani ali pošlje nikamor. Shrani se le fotografija, ki jo sam dodaš h kosu za ponoven razrez.

Kdo vidi podatke

Podatke delavnice vidijo samo člani te delavnice. Če projekt deliš s povezavo za stranko, lahko kdor ima povezavo vidi 3D model in mere omaric tega projekta; povezavo lahko kadar koli ukineš.

Podatki so shranjeni pri ponudniku Supabase v podatkovnem centru v Evropski uniji (Irska), aplikacija pa je gostovana pri Netlify. Oba podatke obdelujeta samo za delovanje aplikacije. Podatkov ne prodajamo, ne uporabljamo za oglase in v aplikaciji ni sledilnikov.

Hramba in izbris

Podatki se hranijo, dokler uporabljaš aplikacijo. Projekte, kose za razrez in opravila lahko izbrišeš sam v aplikaciji. Za izbris celotnega računa ali delavnice piši na mizar.stajl@siol.net; izbrišemo jih v 30 dneh.

Tvoje pravice

Po splošni uredbi o varstvu podatkov (GDPR) imaš pravico do vpogleda, popravka, izbrisa in prenosa svojih podatkov ter pritožbe pri Informacijskem pooblaščencu RS (ip-rs.si).

Privacy policy – Kosmo

Effective 2 November 2026

Kosmo is an app for joinery workshops: scan a part's label and see where it sits in the 3D model. This policy explains what data the app processes and why.

Data we store

  • Account: email and password (stored hashed), and optionally your name, shown to colleagues in the same workshop.
  • Workshop: workshop name and list of members.
  • Projects: files you import (e.g. from 3D Corpus or Mega Tischler) with parts, dimensions, materials and hardware.
  • Workshop activity: which parts were scanned, by whom and when; parts marked for recutting with a note and a photo you add; tasks with due dates.
  • Notifications: if you enable them, a technical address of your device used to deliver notifications.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes and QR codes on labels. Images are processed on the device and are not stored or sent anywhere. Only a photo you deliberately attach to a part for recutting is stored.

Who can see the data

Workshop data is visible only to members of that workshop. If you share a project with a customer link, anyone with the link can view that project's 3D model and cabinet dimensions; you can revoke the link at any time.

Data is stored with Supabase in a data centre in the European Union (Ireland); the app is hosted by Netlify. Both process data only to run the app. We do not sell data, use it for advertising or include trackers.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while you use the app. You can delete projects, recut items and tasks in the app yourself. To delete your whole account or workshop, email mizar.stajl@siol.net; we delete it within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to lodge a complaint with the Slovenian Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si).
